# Check: gapless by ear and no stutter on a slow disk after the prefetch change

A17 (2309d3d) moved the next-track open to a background thread. Play an album with gapless transitions (a live album or a continuous mix): no gap or click between tracks. Then play a playlist from a slow or network disk: the track change must not stutter; if the next file opens too slowly, the track ends and the next one starts normally. Also with RUST_LOG=khz_player=debug under load, xrun counts appear at most every 10 s. Pause with EQ on (all bands +12 dB): CPU use of the audio thread drops back to idle after about 1 s.
