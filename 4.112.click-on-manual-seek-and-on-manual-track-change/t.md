# Click on manual seek and on manual track change

Seen during manual checks on 2026-10-21: clicking the seek bar or switching to another track by hand gives a single audible click. Gapless transitions and pause/resume are clean. Likely cause: on flush the audio callback (src/audio/output.rs fill, the shared.flush branch) drops the ring and sets fade = 0.0 at once, so the output jumps from the current sample to silence with no fade-out; only the 6 ms fade-in after it is smooth. Fix idea: let the engine request a fade-out, have the callback ramp fade to 0 over FADE_SECONDS on the old audio and only then clear the ring and ack the flush (FLUSH_TIMEOUT must cover the extra 6 ms). Also check whether the EQ biquad state should be reset on flush, since its history belongs to the old position. Test: a 440 Hz tone, seek mid-play, the first output samples after the flush request must ramp down, not step.
